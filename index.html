About the Company

Committed to Safety & Excellence

Maa Gyanti Security Services Private Limited is a professionally incorporated private limited company registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Government of India. Based in the state of Bihar, we are dedicated to delivering world-class security and protection services.

Our company is built on the values of integrity, discipline, and unwavering commitment to client safety. From residential complexes to corporate establishments, we provide trained and certified security professionals.
